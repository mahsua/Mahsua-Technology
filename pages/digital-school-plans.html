💳 Payment Process

Transparent and milestone-based payment system for a smooth project journey.

1
20% – Project Initiation

After completing the requirement gathering phase and confirming the project workflow, a payment of 20% of the total cost is required to begin development. Once received, we start the project and provide daily updates on progress.

2
60% – Development Completion

When 80% of the project is complete, we'll share a live testing link for review. The next 60% payment confirms continued development toward final delivery.

3
20% – Final Delivery

Once the project is 100% complete and approved, the final 20% payment is due. After receiving it, we'll deliver the full project code, configure your custom domain, and hand over Admin credentials securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the per-student pricing work?
We calculate pricing based on your total student enrollment. You'll be billed annually for the number of students enrolled at the beginning of the academic year.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Yes, you can upgrade at any time. We'll prorate the cost difference for the remainder of your billing cycle.
Is training included with these plans?
Yes, we provide comprehensive training for your staff with all our plans. The Complete Digital School and Enterprise plans include priority training sessions.
